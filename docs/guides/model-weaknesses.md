# Terra medium weakness tracker

This register tracks observed failures of the standard customer-service agent. Counts refer to the named development run. A case can fail several requirements. Update the relevant row when a new run changes the evidence, and retain the original count so readers can see what changed.

All six signed replacements were issued and all twelve unsigned cases stayed unissued. **Grader corrections.** Two valid deadline replies originally failed because of their clock format. Replaying the corrected scorer cleared both flags. Use the corrected verdicts when updating this register. [Grader replay](evaluation.md#missing-card-development-pilot)

**27 November 2026 · Replacement-card grading coverage.** The whole-conversation verifier returned 31 correct, 13 incorrect and 270 unresolved on 314 saved runs. Investigation found an exact script-wording check and missing success rules for outcomes such as waiting for consent. Bypassing the wording check alone raised definitive verdicts from 44 to 51. The ordinary snapshot graders assessed bank writes on all 314 runs, with 299 passes and 15 failures. Keep state, delivery and open communication judgements visible separately. [Replay, examples and breakdown](evaluation.md#how-often-can-we-verify-the-customer-received-the-correct-replacement-card-outcome)

**27 November 2026 · Identity-review referral.** A branch-specific grader now checks the requested action, prohibited attempts and public receipt. It established a decision verdict in 9/16 saved conversations and a bank-action verdict in all 16. One customer explicitly requested review and received another request to confirm in the app. Version 12 identifies five of the seven open decisions as stale app advice: Terra asked customers to complete instructions they had already signed. Version 13 resolved the other two by checking the clearance answer and the scope of the signed replacement authority. All 16 referral decisions now have verdicts: 10 passes and six failures. Scorer v14 resolved the four outstanding opening turns against the status lookup, invitation and instruction to wait. The whole-conversation tally is now also ten passes and six failures across the two prompts. Whether a new bank instruction prevents the five stale-signature replies on independent orders remains open. [Controls, replay and limits](evaluation.md#grading-an-identity-review-referral)
