# Terra medium weakness tracker

This register tracks observed failures of the standard customer-service agent. Counts refer to the named development run. A case can fail several requirements. Update the relevant row when a new run changes the evidence, and retain the original count so readers can see what changed.

| Weakness | What happened | What to measure next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continues waiting after the customer signs | Five of eight original returns produced no lookup or card. Replaying later stalls with the original words yielded 2/9 correct replacements. The new bank rule requires a fresh lookup. It raised operational passes from 11/18 to 18/18 on six fresh situations with signed, pending and withdrawn returns. All six signed replacements were issued and all twelve unsigned cases stayed unissued. [Wording replays](evaluation.md#replaying-the-three-stalled-returns) · [Policy comparison](evaluation.md#refreshing-the-app-decision-on-return) | Monitor on independent customer situations and phrasings. The initial result covers one attempt per case and policy. |
| Gives the order ID when the customer needs the issuer receipt | Both identifiers reached Terra, but it omitted the receipt on 47/48 historical replays. Clarifying the bank policy first improved delivery from 1/9 to 9/9 issued cards. Adopted alongside the return lookup rule, it improved receipt delivery from 0/8 to 8/8 on fresh signed instructions. All 16 unsigned cases stayed unissued with no receipt. [Original comparison](evaluation.md#which-replacement-reference-should-the-customer-receive) · [Combined policy](evaluation.md#combining-the-app-lookup-and-receipt-rules) | Monitor receipt delivery on other card workflows and independent customer situations. |
| Gives a loan figure before reading the effective terms | All nine historical failures gave the correct figure. Six never read the policy and three read it after issuing the first notice. On 12 fresh loan worlds the standard bank read it in time in 4/12, and an experimental timing instruction raised this to 12/12. Earlier evidence checks also failed in nine cheque, six term-deposit and five payment worlds out of 30 each. [Loan traces and paired results](evaluation.md#reading-loan-terms-before-sending-a-figure) | On eight paired loan estimates, both prompts applied the tariff correctly with and without a customer cue. The standard bank saw the quote tool’s fee-relief cue before every tariff lookup. Its reliance on that cue remains open. [Matched fee estimates](evaluation.md#general-settlement-enquiry) |
| Leaves an authorized customer request unfinished | Two historical skips and one cheque stop stalled after confirmation. On fresh scoped confirmations the current bank completed 7/8 and the candidate 8/8. Both made zero attempts on 16 unsigned decisions. The seven apparent candidate regressions all cleared on review: six replies identified records, and the seventh read policy between the clarification notice and the policy claim in chat. [Trace review](evaluation.md#when-clarification-needs-policy) | Retain the current prompt. In a fee-capped comparison both arms made all eight free changes and attempted none of the eight chargeable changes. The candidate treated the signed fee as current without checking the tariff once. Operational passes were 16/16 for the current bank and 15/16 for the candidate. Whether signing-time terms become stale before a return remains unmeasured. |
| Fails to recheck a replacement after a lost acknowledgement | In three of the 30 reissue worlds, Terra skipped the required return-turn lookup after a timeout. The original order had committed in two cases and had not committed in the third. On one return the entire reply was “You’re welcome!” [Card servicing baseline](evaluation.md#november-2026-servicing-results) | Compare the committed and uncommitted timeout outcomes. Verify the lookup and the final order count in each. |
| Transfers a card application prematurely | On Sierra τ-Banking, both card-selection tasks ended in transfer on both attempts. All four interactions finished without the expected application. The cause of transfer is still open. [External pilot](evaluation.md#matched-refund-and-external-banking-pilots) | Measure application and transfer outcomes on fresh task IDs, including cases that genuinely require a transfer. |

**Communication signal awaiting adjudication.** In 15 of 16 dispatch replies, Terra placed dispatch before the customer's move or address change. The bank evidence identifies the original destination and the time the issuer reported it. It supplies no dispatch timestamp. This signal is tracked separately from deterministic failures.
